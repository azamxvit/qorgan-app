Понятный разбор сообщения
Посмотрите, какие признаки повлияли на оценку: подозрительная ссылка, просьба сообщить секретный код, давление или другие сигналы. Qorğan использует локальные правила и список фишинговых адресов.

Для проверки новых входящих SMS разрешите их приём, разрешите уведомления и включите защиту. Старую переписку Qorğan не загружает. Защиту можно выключить в настройках. Работа предупреждений зависит от разрешений и ограничений Android.

## English

Short description (44 characters; must remain at most 80):

```text

```

Full description:

```text
Qorğan warns about signs of phishing in new SMS messages. After you give permission and enable protection, it checks incoming messages on your phone, including in the background, and can show a warning for a suspicious SMS.

Understand the assessment
See which signals affected the result, such as a suspicious link, a request for a secret code, pressure or other warning signs. Qorğan uses local rules and an on-device phishing-host list.

Manual checking
Paste SMS text to assess it. Manual checking works without SMS permission or an account.

Background checking is your choice
Allow incoming SMS access and notifications, then enable protection. Qorğan does not load your existing SMS inbox. You can turn protection off in settings. Alerts depend on permissions and Android background restrictions.

History on your device
Qorğan keeps up to 100 recent received SMS records, including sender, time and assessment. Clear this history in settings; the original messages remain in your messaging app.

No registration, ads or analytics SDKs
SMS text and senders are not automatically uploaded to a server. The app downloads a public phishing-host list over HTTPS; the hosting infrastructure sees the request's network IP address. The privacy policy explains this processing and voluntary support requests.

Qorğan warns about possible risk. It does not block links or delete SMS from your messaging app. Assessments can be wrong or miss threats. No warning does not establish that a message is authentic. If unsure, contact the organization through an official channel you already know.

The current app interface is in Russian.
Support: omirtayazamatt@gmail.com
```
